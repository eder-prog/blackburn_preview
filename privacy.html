Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026 · Effective: May 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Jonathan Blackburn Construction LLC ("Blackburn Construction," "we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you visit our website, request an estimate, or otherwise interact with us.

1. Information we collect

When you use our estimate request form, we collect:

  • Contact information: your name, phone number, email address, and ZIP code.
  • Project information: project type and any details you choose to share in the message field.
  • Consent records: whether you opted in to contact and/or SMS communications, with a timestamp.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your estimate request and provide a written quote.
  • To schedule a site visit and follow up on the status of your project.
  • To send SMS updates about your estimate or project — only if you opted in (see Section 4).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your information.

3. Sharing with third parties

We share information only with:

  • Service providers who help us run our business (e.g., email provider, scheduling software). These providers are bound by confidentiality.
  • Authorities when required by law (subpoena, court order).

We do not share your information with carriers, marketers, or advertisers.

4. SMS / Text messaging

If you check the optional SMS consent box on our form, you agree to receive text messages from Jonathan Blackburn Construction LLC at the phone number you provided.

  • Message frequency: varies. We typically send 1–4 messages per project, related to your estimate and project status.
  • Message and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier plan.
  • Consent is not a condition of any purchase. You can request an estimate or hire us without opting into SMS.
  • To stop: reply STOP at any time and you will be removed from the SMS list. You will receive a final confirmation message.
  • For help: reply HELP or call (803) 665-7033.

Full SMS Program Terms are available in our Terms of Service.

5. Data retention

We retain your contact and project information for as long as we have an active business relationship with you, plus 7 years after our last contact (for tax and contract record-keeping), unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

6. Your rights

You can:

  • Request a copy of the information we have about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update your information.
  • Ask us to delete your information (subject to legal record-keeping requirements).
  • Opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP.
  • Opt out of email or phone follow-up by emailing info@blackburnconstruction.com.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no electronic transmission or storage system is 100% secure. You share information at your own risk.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. Significant changes will be communicated to active customers by email.
